عيسى عليه السلام

Prophet Isa (Peace be upon him)

The Pure One Spoke as a Baby Full Story for Kids

Many years after Prophet Musa, Allah chose a pure and special woman named Maryam (Mary). She was known for her devotion and goodness. One day while she was alone, the angel Jibril appeared to her in the form of a man.

Maryam was frightened, but the angel said, “I am only a messenger from your Lord to give you the good news of a pure boy.”

A Miraculous Birth

Maryam was surprised. She said, “How can I have a son when no man has touched me?” The angel replied, “It is easy for Allah. He only says ‘Be’ and it is.”

So by the power of Allah alone, Maryam became pregnant and later gave birth to a beautiful baby boy under a palm tree. She was tired and worried about what people would say.

When she returned to her people carrying the baby, they were shocked and started accusing her. Maryam did not argue. She simply pointed to the baby.

Then something amazing happened — the baby Isa spoke clearly from the cradle!

“I am the servant of Allah. He has given me the Book and made me a Prophet. He has made me blessed wherever I am… Peace be upon me the day I was born, the day I die, and the day I will be raised alive.”

Everyone was astonished. How could a newborn baby speak like that? It was a clear miracle from Allah.

The Miracles of Isa

As Isa grew up, Allah gave him many special miracles (by Allah’s permission only):

  • He could heal people who were born blind.
  • He could cure people with serious skin diseases (leprosy).
  • He could even bring the dead back to life — with Allah’s permission.
  • He could make a bird from clay and then breathe into it so it became a real living bird.
  • He told people what they had eaten and what they stored in their houses.

All these miracles were to show people that he was a true Prophet sent by Allah, not to make people worship him.

Important reminder: Prophet Isa always said the miracles happened only by the permission of Allah. He never claimed to be God.

His Message

Prophet Isa called his people to worship only one God — Allah. He taught them to be kind, to give charity, to pray, and to be humble. He confirmed the message of the earlier Prophets and brought a new book called the Injeel (Gospel).

Some people believed him and followed him. They were called his disciples (Hawariyyun). But many of the leaders rejected him and planned to harm him.

Allah Protected Him

When the enemies tried to capture and kill Isa, Allah saved him in a special way. Allah raised Isa up to Himself. The enemies thought they had crucified him, but Allah made it appear so to them. In reality, Isa was not killed and not crucified.

Muslims believe that Prophet Isa is still alive with Allah and will return to earth near the end of time to complete his mission and bring justice.

What We Learn from Prophet Isa

  • Allah can create whatever He wants in any way He chooses.
  • Speaking the truth and calling people to Allah is the job of every Prophet.
  • Miracles come only from Allah — Prophets are His servants.
  • We should never worship any Prophet, no matter how special he is.
  • Allah always protects His true messengers.

Prophet Isa is one of the greatest Prophets and a pure servant of Allah. Whenever you hear his name, remember to say “peace be upon him” and remember that he taught people to worship only the One true God.

Story written with love in simple words by Mustapha Gbanie for every child who wants to grow with beautiful character.
