يونس عليه السلام

Prophet Yunus (Peace be upon him)

The Whale Story Beautiful Dua Full Story for Kids

Prophet Yunus (Jonah) was sent by Allah to a big city called Nineveh. The people there were doing many bad things and refused to worship Allah. Yunus called them to the truth for a long time, but most of them did not listen.

Yunus Leaves the City

One day Yunus became very tired and a little angry with his people. He thought, “They will never believe,” and he left the city without waiting for Allah’s command. He went to the sea and boarded a ship.

While the ship was sailing, a big storm came. The waves became huge and the ship started to sink. The people on the ship said, “Someone on this ship has done something wrong. Let us draw lots to find out who it is.”

The lot fell on Yunus three times. So they threw him into the dark, stormy sea.

Swallowed by a Huge Fish

Allah had prepared a huge fish (a whale) to swallow Yunus. The fish swallowed him whole, but Allah ordered the fish not to harm him. Yunus found himself in complete darkness inside the belly of the fish — the darkness of the night, the darkness of the sea, and the darkness of the fish’s stomach.

In that total darkness, Yunus realised his mistake. He made one of the most beautiful duas in the Quran:

“There is no god but You. Glory be to You! Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.”

He kept repeating this dua with a sincere heart.

Famous dua: This prayer of Yunus is so powerful that many people still recite it today when they are in difficulty. It is called the dua of Yunus.

Allah Saves Him

Allah accepted the dua of Yunus. He commanded the fish to throw Yunus out onto the shore. Yunus was weak and sick, but alive.

Allah made a special plant (a gourd plant) grow over him to give him shade and comfort while he recovered.

After Yunus became strong again, Allah sent him back to his people. This time something wonderful happened — more than one hundred thousand people believed in Allah! They all accepted the message of Yunus.

What We Learn from Prophet Yunus

  • Never leave your responsibility without Allah’s permission.
  • When you make a mistake, turn back to Allah immediately with a sincere heart.
  • The dua of Yunus is a powerful prayer for anyone in difficulty.
  • Allah can save a person even from the belly of a huge fish.
  • Even if people reject you at first, keep hoping — they may accept later.

Whenever you feel trapped or scared, remember the dua of Prophet Yunus and say it with full trust in Allah. He hears every sincere prayer.
