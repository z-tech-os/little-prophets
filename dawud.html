داود عليه السلام

Prophet Dawud (Peace be upon him)

King & Prophet Beautiful Voice Full Story for Kids

Prophet Dawud (David) started as a young shepherd boy. He was brave, kind, and loved to worship Allah. Allah gave him a special gift — a very beautiful voice. When he recited the Zabur (Psalms), the mountains and birds would join him in praising Allah!

The Giant Challenge

One day a huge and strong enemy warrior named Jalut (Goliath) challenged the army of the believers. Jalut was so big and scary that many soldiers were afraid to fight him.

Young Dawud stepped forward and said he would fight the giant. People were surprised because Dawud was still a young man and not a trained soldier. But Dawud trusted Allah completely.

He took only a sling and some stones. With one precise throw, he hit Jalut and defeated him. The whole army of the enemy ran away. Everyone was amazed at the courage of this young man.

Lesson: Size and strength do not matter when Allah is with you. A small stone with strong faith can defeat a giant.

Becoming a King and Prophet

Because of his bravery and good character, Allah made Dawud both a Prophet and a king. He ruled with justice and wisdom. People loved him because he was fair and always remembered Allah.

Allah also taught Dawud how to make strong armour from iron. He could soften iron with his hands (by Allah’s permission) and shape it into protective clothing for soldiers.

A Lesson in Judgement

One day two people came to Dawud with a problem about sheep. Dawud listened carefully and made a fair decision. Later his son Sulaiman (who was still young) gave an even wiser suggestion. Dawud smiled and accepted his son’s better idea. This showed that even a great Prophet and king can listen to good advice from others.

What We Learn from Prophet Dawud

  • Courage comes from trusting Allah, not from being big or strong.
  • A beautiful voice should be used to praise Allah.
  • A good leader is fair, just, and listens to wise advice.
  • Even when you become important, stay humble.
  • Allah can give special skills to those who serve Him.

Prophet Dawud teaches us that a young person with strong faith can do great things for Allah and for people.

Story written with love in simple words by Mustapha Gbanie for every child who wants to grow with beautiful character.
