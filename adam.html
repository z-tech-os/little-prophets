Prophet Adam (Peace be upon him)

The First Human The First Prophet Full Story for Kids

Long, long ago — before there were any people, any houses, any cars, or even any trees on earth — Allah decided to create the very first human. His name was Adam. Can you say it with me? A-dam!

How Allah Created Adam

Allah took special clay from different parts of the earth. Some clay was soft, some was a bit hard, some was dark, and some was light. That is why people today have different skin colours — we all come from the same clay that Allah mixed together!

Then Allah shaped the clay into the form of a man. He made him tall and strong. After that, Allah blew a special spirit into him… and Adam opened his eyes! The first human was alive.

Fun fact for kids: When the angels first saw Adam, some of them were surprised. But Allah already knew that humans would be special.

Allah Taught Adam Many Things

Allah taught Adam the names of everything — the names of animals, plants, the sky, the stars, and even feelings. Adam became very clever because Allah was his teacher!

One day Allah asked the angels, “Can you tell Me the names of these things?” The angels said, “We only know what You taught us.” Then Allah asked Adam, and Adam told all the names correctly. The angels were amazed. They realised that Allah had given humans a special gift — the ability to learn!

Living in the Beautiful Garden

Allah placed Adam in a wonderful garden called Paradise (Jannah). It was full of delicious fruits, soft grass, and sparkling rivers. Everything Adam needed was there.

Later Allah created a partner for Adam so he would not feel lonely. Her name was Hawwa (Eve). Together they lived happily in the garden. Allah told them, “You may eat from any tree you like… except this one special tree. Do not go near it.”

Remember this rule: When Allah says “Do not do this”, it is always for our own good — even if we do not understand why yet.

The Big Mistake

There was a very jealous creature called Iblis (Shaytan). He hated that Allah had honoured Adam. So he whispered to Adam and Hawwa, “Why don’t you taste the fruit from that special tree? If you eat it, you will become like angels or live forever!”

Adam and Hawwa forgot Allah’s warning for a moment and tasted the fruit. Suddenly they felt ashamed and sad. They realised they had made a big mistake.

But here is the beautiful part: Adam did not run away or make excuses. He and Hawwa said, “Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves. If You do not forgive us, we will be lost.”

Allah is the Most Forgiving. He accepted their apology (tawbah) and forgave them. Then He sent them to live on earth for a while, where they would learn, grow, and teach their children about Allah.

Life on Earth

On earth, Adam became the first Prophet. He taught his children to worship only Allah, to be kind, and to stay away from the tricks of Shaytan. From Adam and Hawwa came all the people of the world — including you and me!

What We Learn from Prophet Adam

  • Allah created us in the best way and gave us the ability to learn.
  • When we make a mistake, the best thing is to say sorry to Allah quickly.
  • Allah loves those who repent and ask for forgiveness.
  • We should never follow the whispers of Shaytan.
  • Every human being is part of one big family that started with Adam.

So next time you see different people with different colours and languages, remember: we all come from the same father — Prophet Adam (peace be upon him). That is why we should always be kind to one another.

Story written with love in simple words by Mustapha Gbanie for every child who wants to grow with beautiful character.
