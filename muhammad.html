محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

The Final Messenger Mercy to All Worlds Full Story for Kids

The last and greatest Prophet is our beloved Muhammad ﷺ. He is the final Messenger sent by Allah to all people until the end of time. His life is the best example for every human being — children and adults.

A Special Birth

Muhammad ﷺ was born in the city of Makkah in the Year of the Elephant. His father, Abdullah, passed away before he was born. His mother, Aminah, also passed away when he was only six years old. So he became an orphan at a very young age.

First his grandfather Abdul Muttalib took care of him. After the grandfather died, his uncle Abu Talib looked after him with great love. Even as a child, Muhammad ﷺ was known for being honest, kind, and helpful.

His special nickname: People called him Al-Amin — the Trustworthy One — because he never lied and always kept his promises.

Growing Up Honest

As a young man, Muhammad ﷺ worked as a merchant. He was so honest in business that everyone trusted him. A noble lady named Khadijah hired him to take her goods to Syria. When he returned with great profit and a perfect reputation, Khadijah was very impressed. Later she asked to marry him. They had a happy marriage and she became the first person to believe in his message.

The First Revelation

When Muhammad ﷺ was forty years old, he loved to go to a quiet cave called Hira to think about Allah and the people who worshipped idols.

One night in the month of Ramadan, the angel Jibril appeared to him and said: “Read!” (Iqra). Muhammad ﷺ said, “I cannot read.” The angel hugged him tightly and repeated, “Read!” Then the first verses of the Quran were revealed:

“Read in the name of your Lord who created… Created man from a clinging clot. Read! And your Lord is the Most Generous.”

Muhammad ﷺ was frightened at first and ran home to Khadijah. She comforted him and took him to her cousin Waraqah, who confirmed that this was the same angel who came to Prophet Musa.

Calling People to Allah

For the first three years, Muhammad ﷺ invited people privately. Then Allah commanded him to call everyone openly. He climbed a hill and called the people of Makkah. He told them: “Worship only one God — Allah. Stop worshipping idols. Be kind, honest, and just.”

Some people believed him quickly — like Abu Bakr, Ali, and many of the poor and weak. But many of the leaders rejected him. They laughed, insulted him, and hurt the Muslims. Still, the Prophet ﷺ remained patient and kind.

The Migration to Madinah

After thirteen hard years in Makkah, Allah allowed the Muslims to migrate to a city called Yathrib (later named Madinah). This journey is called the Hijrah. In Madinah, the Prophet ﷺ built the first mosque, made a beautiful brotherhood between the helpers (Ansar) and the migrants (Muhajirun), and created a peaceful community based on justice and mercy.

The Best Character

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was the kindest person. He smiled often, played with children, helped with housework, visited the sick, and forgave people who hurt him. When the people of Makkah who had tortured the Muslims finally surrendered, the Prophet ﷺ forgave almost all of them and said:

“Go, you are free.”

He never took revenge for himself. His mercy was for everyone — Muslims, non-Muslims, animals, and even trees.

The Final Message

Allah revealed the complete Quran to him over 23 years. The Quran is the final book of guidance for all humanity. Before he passed away at the age of 63, the Prophet ﷺ completed the religion. On the day of his farewell pilgrimage he said:

“I have left among you two things. If you hold on to them, you will never go astray: the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Prophet.”

What We Learn from Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

  • Honesty and trustworthiness are the foundations of a good character.
  • Be kind to children, the poor, the weak, and even animals.
  • Patience during hard times brings great reward.
  • Forgiving others (even enemies) is a sign of true strength.
  • The best way to live is to follow the Quran and the example of the Prophet ﷺ.
  • He is a mercy to all the worlds — so we should also try to be merciful.

Every time we say or hear the name of Prophet Muhammad, we should say ﷺ (sallallahu alayhi wa sallam) — “May the peace and blessings of Allah be upon him.”

He is our best teacher and the perfect example until the end of time. May we all try to follow his beautiful manners. Ameen.
