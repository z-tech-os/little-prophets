Prophet Nuh (Peace be upon him)

The Ark Builder 950 Years of Patience Full Story for Kids

Many, many years after Prophet Adam, people on earth started forgetting Allah. They made statues from wood and stone and started praying to them instead of the One true God. That made Allah very sad, because He loves His creation.

So Allah chose a kind and patient man named Nuh (Noah) to be a Prophet. Nuh’s job was to remind everyone: “Worship only Allah! Stop praying to statues!”

Nuh Calls the People

Prophet Nuh started talking to people day and night. He said, “My people! I am a clear warner to you. Worship Allah, fear Him, and obey me. He will forgive your sins and give you more time to do good.”

But most people laughed at him. The rich and important people said, “You are just a normal man like us. Why should we listen to you? Only the poor and weak follow you!”

Nuh did not get angry. He kept inviting them for a very, very long time — some scholars say he called his people for 950 years! Can you imagine being patient for almost one thousand years?

Wow fact: Prophet Nuh is one of the five greatest Prophets (Ulul Azm). His patience is a huge example for all of us.

Allah Commands Nuh to Build a Boat

After so many years, only a small number of people believed in Nuh. Allah told him, “Build a big boat (an Ark) under Our watch and with Our inspiration. Do not speak to Me about those who have done wrong — they will be drowned.”

So Nuh started building a huge wooden boat on dry land. People walked by and laughed even more. They said, “Nuh, have you become a carpenter now? Why are you building a boat where there is no water? You must be crazy!”

Nuh answered calmly, “You will soon know who is really laughing and who is being laughed at.”

The Great Flood

When the boat was ready, Allah told Nuh to take on board:

  • His family who believed
  • All the believers (there were not many)
  • A pair (male and female) of every kind of animal

Then the sky opened with heavy rain, and water gushed up from the ground. The flood became so big that even the mountains were covered. The big boat floated safely on the water while everything else was drowned.

Important point: One of Nuh’s own sons refused to come on the boat. He said, “I will climb a mountain and be safe.” But no mountain could save him. Being related to a Prophet does not automatically save a person — believing and obeying Allah does.

The Flood Ends

After a long time, Allah commanded the sky to stop raining and the earth to swallow the water. The boat came to rest on a mountain called Judi. Nuh and the believers stepped out onto a clean earth. Life started again with people who worshipped only Allah.

Allah told Nuh, “O Nuh, come down from the Ark with peace from Us and blessings upon you and upon the people who are with you.”

What We Learn from Prophet Nuh

  • Keep calling people to good even if they laugh at you.
  • Patience is a powerful strength — Nuh waited almost 950 years!
  • Obeying Allah brings safety, even when the whole world seems against you.
  • Being the child of a good person is not enough; we must choose the right path ourselves.
  • Allah always protects the believers in the end.

So whenever something feels hard and takes a long time, remember Prophet Nuh and his big boat. Keep doing the right thing, and Allah will take care of the rest.
