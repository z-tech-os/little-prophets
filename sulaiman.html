سليمان عليه السلام

Prophet Sulaiman (Peace be upon him)

Spoke to Animals Controlled the Wind Full Story for Kids

Prophet Sulaiman (Solomon) was the son of Prophet Dawud. Allah gave him something very special: wisdom, a great kingdom, and the ability to understand the language of animals and birds!

Talking to the Creatures

One day Sulaiman was marching with his army of humans, jinn, and birds. He heard an ant saying to the other ants, “O ants, go into your homes so that Sulaiman and his army do not crush you without noticing!”

Sulaiman smiled and thanked Allah for this special gift. He was careful not to harm any creature.

Amazing gift: Sulaiman could understand what birds and animals were saying. He even had a special hoopoe bird that brought him important news.

The Queen of Sheba

One day the hoopoe bird told Sulaiman about a rich kingdom ruled by a queen (Bilqis). The people there worshipped the sun instead of Allah.

Sulaiman sent a letter inviting the queen to worship the One true God. The queen was wise. She decided to visit Sulaiman herself.

Before she arrived, Sulaiman asked his helpers to bring her magnificent throne to his palace in the blink of an eye. When the queen came and saw her own throne, she was amazed. She realised the power of Allah and accepted the true faith.

A Just and Wise King

Sulaiman ruled a huge kingdom with justice. The wind would carry him wherever he wanted (by Allah’s permission). Jinn worked for him and built beautiful buildings. Yet Sulaiman remained humble and always thanked Allah for these blessings.

He never became proud. He knew that all power belongs only to Allah.

What We Learn from Prophet Sulaiman

  • Wisdom is better than wealth or power.
  • Be kind to all creatures — even the smallest ants.
  • Use the gifts Allah gives you to call people to the truth.
  • Stay humble even when you have great success.
  • A wise leader invites people with good manners, not force.

Prophet Sulaiman shows us that real greatness is in using Allah’s gifts to serve people and to guide them to the truth.

Story written with love in simple words by Mustapha Gbanie for every child who wants to grow with beautiful character.
