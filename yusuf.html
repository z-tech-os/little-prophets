يوسف عليه السلام

Prophet Yusuf (Peace be upon him)

The Dreamer Master of Forgiveness Full Story for Kids

Once upon a time there was a young boy named Yusuf. He had eleven brothers and a loving father named Prophet Ya’qub (Jacob). Yusuf was very handsome, kind, and his father loved him a lot.

The Amazing Dream

One night Yusuf had a special dream. He saw eleven stars, the sun, and the moon — all bowing down to him! He told his father about the dream. His father smiled and said, “My dear son, this is a special dream from Allah. Do not tell your brothers about it, or they might become jealous.”

But somehow the brothers found out. They already felt that their father loved Yusuf more than them. The dream made them even more jealous and angry.

The Brothers’ Bad Plan

One day the brothers said to their father, “Let Yusuf come with us to play and run in the fields. We will take good care of him.” Their father was worried, but he finally allowed it.

When they were far away, the brothers threw Yusuf into a deep, dark well. Then they killed a goat, put its blood on Yusuf’s shirt, and went home crying. They told their father, “A wolf ate Yusuf!” Their father was heartbroken, but he said, “I will be patient and ask Allah for help.”

Lesson already starting: Jealousy is a very dangerous feeling. It can make people do very bad things.

From the Well to Egypt

Later, a group of travellers stopped at the well to get water. They pulled Yusuf out and were surprised to find a beautiful boy. They took him to Egypt and sold him as a servant to a kind official called Al-Aziz.

Yusuf worked hard and was honest. Allah gave him knowledge and the ability to understand dreams. He grew into a fine young man.

The Hard Test

The wife of Al-Aziz tried to make Yusuf do something wrong. Yusuf refused and said, “I seek protection from Allah!” Because of this, he was put in prison even though he had done nothing wrong.

In prison, Yusuf stayed patient and kept teaching people about Allah. Two prisoners had strange dreams, and Yusuf explained them correctly. One of them was later freed and became the king’s servant.

The King’s Dream

One night the King of Egypt had a strange dream: seven fat cows being eaten by seven thin cows, and seven green ears of corn next to seven dry ones. No one could explain it — until the servant remembered Yusuf.

Yusuf was brought from prison. He explained: “There will be seven years of good harvest, then seven years of hard famine. You should store food carefully during the good years.”

The king was so impressed that he made Yusuf the minister in charge of all the food stores of Egypt. From a boy in a well, Yusuf became a trusted leader!

The Brothers Come Back

When the famine came, Yusuf’s brothers travelled to Egypt to buy food. They did not recognise him, but Yusuf recognised them. He was kind to them and gave them food. After a few visits and some clever tests, Yusuf finally told them who he was.

The brothers were shocked and scared. They expected Yusuf to punish them. But Yusuf said one of the most beautiful sentences in the Quran:

“No blame will there be upon you today. Allah will forgive you. He is the Most Merciful of the merciful.”

He forgave them completely and invited the whole family to come and live in Egypt in honour and safety. His father Ya’qub’s eyes, which had become blind from crying, were healed when he smelled Yusuf’s shirt.

What We Learn from Prophet Yusuf

  • Jealousy can destroy families — always fight against it.
  • Be patient when bad things happen. Allah has a plan.
  • Stay honest and pure even when no one is watching.
  • Forgiving others (especially family) is a sign of great strength.
  • Allah can raise a person from a deep well to a high position.

The story of Yusuf is one of the most beautiful stories in the Quran. Whenever you feel treated unfairly, remember the boy who was thrown into a well and later became a leader who forgave everyone.

Story written with love in simple words by Mustapha Gbanie for every child who wants to grow with beautiful character.
