A message from the heart

Why This Website Exists

A simple story of hope, love for children, and the desire to leave something beautiful behind.

Assalamu Alaikum, dear parents and little readers.

My name is Mustapha Gbanie. Right now, as I build this website, I am broke. I have no job. I have no wife yet. What I do have is my skills, my persistent learning, and a very big hope in my heart.

I dream of the day Allah will bless me with a happy family. I dream of sitting with my own children one day and telling them the stories of the Prophets — not just short summaries, but the full, beautiful stories so they can really understand, laugh at the funny parts, feel the emotions, and take the lessons into their lives.

Why I Love Kids So Much

I love children. Their pure hearts, their questions, their laughter — everything. I want every child who grows up reading these stories to have good character: to be honest like Prophet Yusuf, patient like Prophet Ayyub, brave like Prophet Musa, kind like Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, and always trusting in Allah like all the Prophets.

Even before I have my own children, I want the children who are already in this world to benefit. If a parent somewhere reads these stories to their little ones and the child grows up with better manners and stronger faith, then my effort is already successful.

That is why I created Little Prophets.

I wrote the stories in very simple language and a playful tone so that children understand quickly and even smile while learning. Every story is the full story (not just a tiny summary) so that parents never feel they are “lying” or leaving out important parts. At the end of each story you will find clear “What We Learn” points — easy lessons children can remember and practice.

My Hope

One day, insha’Allah, when I have my own family, I will open this same website and read these exact stories to my children. Until that day comes, this website is my gift to every parent who wants the same good character for their kids, and to every child who needs beautiful mentors from the lives of the Prophets.

If these stories help even one child become more patient, more honest, more kind, or closer to Allah — then every late night I spent building this site was worth it.

Thank you for visiting. Please share these stories with the children in your life. May Allah accept this small effort, put barakah in it, and make it a continuous charity (sadaqah jariyah) for me and for everyone who benefits from it.

With love and hope,
Mustapha Gbanie
