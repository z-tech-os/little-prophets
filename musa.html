موسى عليه السلام

Prophet Musa (Peace be upon him)

The Sea Split Spoke to Allah Full Story for Kids

Long ago in Egypt there lived a very powerful and cruel king called Fir’awn (Pharaoh). He made the Children of Israel (Banu Israel) work as slaves and treated them very badly. One day he heard that a special boy would be born who would take away his power. So he ordered that every newborn baby boy of the Israelites should be killed.

A Baby in a Basket

In those scary days, a mother gave birth to a beautiful baby boy. She was terrified. Allah inspired her to put the baby in a strong basket and gently place it on the river Nile. She did so with a heavy heart, trusting Allah completely.

The basket floated and was found by the family of Fir’awn himself! Fir’awn’s wife was a kind and believing woman named Asiya. She looked at the baby and said, “This child will be a joy for me and you. Do not kill him.” So the baby was raised in the palace of the very king who wanted to kill him! His name was Musa.

Amazing plan of Allah: The baby who was supposed to be killed was raised in the enemy’s own house. Allah’s plans are always the best!

The Burning Bush

When Musa grew up, he left Egypt for a while. One day while travelling with his family, he saw a fire on the side of a mountain (Mount Tur). He went closer and heard a voice:

“O Musa! I am Allah, the Lord of all the worlds. Take off your shoes. You are in the sacred valley.”

Allah spoke directly to Musa! He gave him two miracles: 1. His stick could turn into a real snake. 2. His hand would shine with a bright white light.

Allah then ordered him: “Go to Fir’awn. He has become a great tyrant. Tell him to free the Children of Israel and to worship only Allah.”

Facing the Powerful King

Musa and his brother Harun (Aaron) went to Fir’awn. They showed him the miracles. Fir’awn was scared but too proud to believe. He called his best magicians to compete with Musa.

The magicians threw their ropes and sticks, and they looked like snakes. Then Musa threw his stick — it became a real huge snake and swallowed all the fake ones! The magicians immediately realised this was not magic. They fell down in sujood and said, “We believe in the Lord of Musa and Harun!”

Fir’awn became even angrier and punished the magicians, but they stayed firm in their faith.

The Great Miracle – The Sea Splits

Finally Allah told Musa to take the Children of Israel and leave Egypt at night. When Fir’awn heard about it, he gathered a huge army and chased them.

The people reached the edge of the Red Sea. In front of them was the deep water. Behind them was Fir’awn’s army. They cried, “We are trapped!”

Musa said with full trust:

“No! My Lord is with me. He will guide me.”

Then Allah commanded Musa to strike the sea with his stick. Instantly the sea split into two huge walls of water, with a dry path in the middle! The believers walked safely across. When Fir’awn and his army tried to follow, the sea closed back over them and they drowned.

The Children of Israel were finally free.

The Torah and the Mountain

Later Allah called Musa to Mount Tur again and gave him the Torah (Tawrah) — a holy book with guidance and laws for his people. Musa spent many years teaching his people to worship only Allah and to live with good manners.

What We Learn from Prophet Musa

  • Allah can protect a baby even in the palace of an enemy.
  • Speaking the truth to powerful people takes courage — and Allah helps the brave.
  • When you feel trapped, trust Allah. He can make a way where there seems to be no way.
  • Magic and tricks can never defeat the real power of Allah.
  • Patience and strong faith can free a whole nation.

Whenever you feel scared or stuck, remember Prophet Musa standing in front of the sea and saying with certainty: “My Lord is with me.”

Story written with love in simple words by Mustapha Gbanie for every child who wants to grow with beautiful character.
